Rally Room

Table tennis against real people. Start a table, share the four-letter code, and play singles, doubles or one against two.

Match type

Move the paddle with your mouse or finger and meet the ball after it bounces. The faster you swing forward, the faster the shot and the more topspin. Pull back for a backspin chop, swipe sideways for sidespin. Heavy topspin pops your return up and long, so chop it or keep your swing soft. A backspin ball has to be lifted with a forward topspin swing or chopped back, or your return drops into the net. First to 11, win by two.
